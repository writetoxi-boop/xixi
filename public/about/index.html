Our Story

XIXI was born from a conviction: that Asian culture, creativity, and capital are not a niche story — they are the story of our era.

We exist to change that. XIXI is a Gen Z luxury media platform spotlighting the forces shaping Asia and, by extension, the world. Fashion that bends the rules. Art that refuses translation. Founders that move faster than the press can write.

"The world is looking East. We've been here the whole time."

We publish across five verticals — Fashion, Art, Culture, Capital, and Power — and produce The Asia Letter, read by 12,000+ globally curious readers every week.
